# Code of conduct

Be kind, be patient, and assume good faith. People come here with very different levels of experience in machining, electronics and software.

- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Safety advice is welcome; mocking someone for a safety mistake is not.

If someone's behaviour is a problem, open a private report through the repository's **Security > Report a vulnerability** form or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Maintainers may remove comments or block accounts that ignore this.

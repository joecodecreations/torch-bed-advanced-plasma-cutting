# Roadmap

Roughly in order of how much they matter for real cutting.

- **Hardware validation.** Real cuts and real flashes, with the results fed back into defaults and docs.
- **Kerf compensation**, inside and outside, using the per-material cut width.
- **Lead-ins and lead-outs** so pierce marks land in scrap.
- **Torch height control** (a Z axis, and ohmic or arc-voltage sensing).
- **Limit switches and homing** wired into the workflow.
- **True-shape nesting**, including parts inside the holes of other parts.
- **Alignment holes** through stacked 3D layers so they can be pinned before welding.
- **Three-direction slicing.**
- **Editable curves** after a pen path is finished.
- **More controllers** beyond GRBL on the ATmega328P.

Have a different priority? Open an issue.
